## Repairs

Repairs have a 1-year warranty on parts and labor. If the same repair fails within that year, we fix it at no cost.

## New installs

New installs have a 10-year parts warranty from the manufacturer when the equipment is registered within 60 days. We register the equipment for you. We also give a 2-year labor warranty.

## How to use it

Send us a message in the chat and say it is a warranty visit. Have your invoice date ready if you can.
