These are our typical installed prices.

## Prices

- New central AC system with a matching furnace or air handler: $6,500 to $14,000
- Heat pump system: $8,000 to $16,000
- Furnace alone: $3,500 to $7,500

## What is included

The price includes the city permit, haul-away of the old equipment and a load calculation. The load calculation sizes the system to your home.

## Getting an exact price

Every install starts with a free in-home estimate. The visit takes about 1 hour, and you get a written quote with 3 options.

## Paying for it

Financing is available for jobs over $2,500, with approved credit.
