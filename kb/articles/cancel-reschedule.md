title: How do I cancel or reschedule?
topic: booking
---

You can cancel or reschedule for free up to 24 hours before your arrival window.

## Late changes

Changes made less than 24 hours ahead have a $50 late change fee. This covers the time slot we held for you.

## How to change

Send us a message in the chat with your name and the visit you want to change. The chat shows you new open times.

## Missed visits

If no one is home during the 2-hour arrival window, the visit counts as a late change and the $50 late change fee applies.
