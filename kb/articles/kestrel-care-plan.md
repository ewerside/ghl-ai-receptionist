topic: maintenance
---

The Kestrel Care plan is our maintenance plan. It costs $19 per month.

## What you get

- 2 tune-ups per year, one in spring and one in fall
- 15% off repairs
- No diagnostic fee
- Priority booking

## Is it worth it

Two tune-ups cost $119 each without the plan. Members also get 15% off repairs and pay no diagnostic fee.

## How to join

Ask in the chat or ask the technician at your next visit.
