Yes. Emergency visits are available 24/7.

## What counts as an emergency

- No cooling when it is above 90°F outside
- No heat when it is below 40°F outside
- An active water leak
- A sewer backup

## How fast we come

Emergencies get the first open visit today or tomorrow. The chat offers you that first open time right away.

## Cost

An emergency visit has the same $89 diagnostic fee as any repair visit. If you approve the repair, the $89 fee is waived. Visits outside regular hours (evenings, Sundays and holidays) have a $149 after-hours fee on top of the $89 diagnostic fee.

## While you wait

For an active leak, turn off the main water valve. If the AC is out on a hot day, close the blinds and run fans.
