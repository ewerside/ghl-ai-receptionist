## Paying

Payment is due when the work is done. We take credit and debit cards, checks and bank transfer.

## Discounts

Veterans and seniors aged 65 and over get 10% off repairs. Kestrel Care members get 15% off repairs.

## Questions about a bill

Questions about a bill, a refund or a complaint go to our office team. The chat passes your message to them, and they reply within 1 business day.

## Complaints

If something went wrong with a visit, tell us in the chat. The office team reads every complaint and replies within 1 business day.
