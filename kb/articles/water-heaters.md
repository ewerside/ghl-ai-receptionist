title: Do you repair and replace water heaters?
topic: plumbing
---

Yes. We work on tank and tankless water heaters.

## Prices

- Water heater repair: $200 to $650
- New tank water heater: $1,800 to $3,200 installed
- New tankless water heater: $3,500 to $6,000 installed

## Signs of a problem

- No hot water or not enough hot water
- Water around the base of the tank
- Rumbling or popping sounds

## Leaks

Water leaking from the tank is an active water leak. Turn off the main water valve and book an emergency visit. Emergencies get the first open visit today or tomorrow.

## Warranty

Repairs have a 1-year warranty on parts and labor.
