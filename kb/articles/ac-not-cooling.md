title: My AC is not cooling. What can I check first?
topic: emergency
---

A few checks can save you a visit.

## Quick checks

1. Set the thermostat to cool and a few degrees below room temperature.
2. Replace the thermostat batteries if the screen is blank.
3. Check the breaker for the outdoor unit and the indoor unit.
4. Look at the air filter. A clogged filter can block the airflow.
5. Make sure the outdoor unit is not covered by leaves or grass.

## When to call us

If the AC still does not cool, book a repair visit. A repair visit has an $89 diagnostic fee, and the fee is waived if you approve the repair.

## Hot days

No cooling when it is above 90°F outside counts as an emergency. Emergencies get the first open visit today or tomorrow.
