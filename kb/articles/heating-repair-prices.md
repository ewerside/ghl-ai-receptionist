Most heating repairs fall in these ranges.

## Typical prices

- Igniter replacement: $150 to $400
- Furnace repair: $200 to $900
- Heat pump repair: $250 to $1,200

## How the price is set

A repair visit has an $89 diagnostic fee. The technician gives you a written price before any work starts. If you approve the repair, the $89 fee is waived.

## No heat on a cold day

No heat when it is below 40°F outside counts as an emergency. Emergencies get the first open visit today or tomorrow.

## Gas smell

If you smell gas, leave the home first and call 911 and your gas company from outside.
