Regular appointments run Monday to Saturday, 8 am to 6 pm Central Time.

## Sundays and holidays

We are closed for regular appointments on Sundays and on major holidays. Emergency visits are still available 24/7. Visits outside regular hours have a $149 after-hours fee on top of the $89 diagnostic fee.

## The chat

The chat on our website answers questions and books visits 24/7. You can book on a Sunday night for a visit on Monday morning.

## Arrival windows

Every visit is booked as a 2-hour arrival window. The first window of the day starts at 8 am.
