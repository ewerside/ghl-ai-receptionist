We hire HVAC technicians and plumbers.

## How to apply

Send your resume to jobs@kestrel.example.com. The chat cannot take job applications.

## What we look for

- A Texas license, or progress toward one
- A clean driving record
- Care for customers and their homes

## What we offer

Our technicians work Monday to Saturday, 8 am to 6 pm, with a rotation for emergency visits.
