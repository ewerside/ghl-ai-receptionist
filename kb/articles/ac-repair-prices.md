Most AC repairs fall in these ranges.

## Typical prices

- Capacitor replacement: $150 to $350
- Refrigerant leak repair: $400 to $1,500
- Blower motor replacement: $450 to $900
- Compressor replacement: $1,800 to $3,500

## How the price is set

A repair visit has an $89 diagnostic fee. The technician finds the problem and gives you a written price before any work starts. If you approve the repair, the $89 fee is waived.

## Repair or replace

If the compressor fails on an older system, a new system can make more sense. The technician will tell you both options. A new central AC system costs $6,500 to $14,000 installed.

## Warranty

Repairs have a 1-year warranty on parts and labor.
