title: I rent my home. Can I book a visit?
topic: install
---

It depends on the service.

## Repairs

Tenants can book repair visits. A repair visit has an $89 diagnostic fee. Check your lease, because many landlords pay for repairs.

## Installs and replacements

Installs and system replacements need the property owner to request the estimate and sign the quote. Tenants can ask their landlord or property manager to contact us.

## Why

A new system changes the property and needs a city permit. Only the property owner can approve that work.

## Tune-ups

Tenants can book tune-ups. A tune-up costs $119 per system.
