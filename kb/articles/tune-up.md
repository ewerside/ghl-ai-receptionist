topic: maintenance

A tune-up costs $119 per system.

## When

We recommend one for cooling in spring and one for heating in fall.

## What the technician does

- Cleans the outdoor coil and checks the refrigerant
- Checks the electrical parts and the capacitor
- Checks the drain line
- Tests the system and tells you about any problem found

## Repairs found during a tune-up

If the technician finds a problem, you get a written price before any work starts.

## Save with a plan

The Kestrel Care plan costs $19 per month and includes 2 tune-ups per year.
