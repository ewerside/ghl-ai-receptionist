title: What should I do if I smell gas?
topic: emergency
---

If you smell gas, leave the home first. Then call 911 and your gas company from outside.

## Do not

- Do not use light switches or the thermostat.
- Do not start a car in an attached garage.
- Do not try to find the leak yourself.

## After the gas company

Once the gas company says the home is safe, we can repair the furnace, water heater or gas line that caused the problem. Book a repair visit in the chat.

## Water leaks

For an active leak, turn off the main water valve while you wait for us. The main valve is often near the water meter or where the pipe enters the house. An active water leak counts as an emergency, and emergencies get the first open visit today or tomorrow.
