title: How does the free install estimate work?
topic: install
---

Every install starts with a free in-home estimate. There is no diagnostic fee.

## The visit

The visit takes about 1 hour. The technician measures your home, checks your ductwork and runs a load calculation.

## The quote

You get a written quote with 3 options. Each option shows the equipment, the installed price and the warranty.

## Who can book

Installs and system replacements need the property owner to request the estimate and sign the quote.

## Booking

Every visit is booked as a 2-hour arrival window. Tell the chat your ZIP code and that you own the home, and it will offer you times.
