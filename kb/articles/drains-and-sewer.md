Yes.

## Prices

- Drain clearing: $175 to $350
- Sewer camera inspection: $250

## Sewer backup

A sewer backup counts as an emergency. Signs are water coming up in a tub or shower drain, or more than one drain backing up at once. Emergencies get the first open visit today or tomorrow.

## Camera inspection

If drains keep backing up, a sewer camera inspection shows roots, breaks or buildup inside the line. You get the video and a written price for the repair.
