title: Do you offer financing?
topic: payment
---

Yes. Financing is available for jobs over $2,500, with approved credit.

## Options

- 0% interest for 18 months
- Fixed monthly payments for 60 to 120 months

## How to apply

The technician can start the application during your free in-home estimate. You get an answer before you sign the quote.

## What it covers

Financing covers new AC, heat pump and furnace systems, water heaters and large repairs over $2,500.
