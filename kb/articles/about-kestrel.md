Kestrel Air & Plumbing repairs, maintains and installs air conditioning, heating and plumbing in the Austin area.

## Homes only

We work on homes only. We do not take jobs in offices, stores or other commercial buildings.

## Brands

We service all major brands of AC units, furnaces, heat pumps and water heaters. You do not need to know the brand before you book. The technician checks it at the visit.

## Our team

Our technicians are licensed and insured. Every visit is booked as a 2-hour arrival window, so you know when to expect us.

## What we do

- AC repair, tune-ups and new systems
- Furnace and heat pump repair, tune-ups and new systems
- Water heaters, drains, sewer lines and leaks

## How to reach us

The chat on our website answers questions and books visits 24/7. Regular appointments run Monday to Saturday, 8 am to 6 pm Central Time.

## Talking to a person

If you want to talk to a person, or the chat cannot answer your question, the chat passes your message to our office team. They reply within 1 business day.
