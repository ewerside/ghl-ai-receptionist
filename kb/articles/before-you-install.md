A new system is a big purchase. Here is what to think about first.

## Size

A system that is too big or too small wastes energy. Every estimate includes a load calculation to size the system to your home.

## Ductwork

Old or leaking ducts can waste part of the air you pay to cool. The technician checks your ducts during the estimate.

## Who signs

Installs and system replacements need the property owner to request the estimate and sign the quote.

## Permit

Our price includes the city permit and haul-away of the old equipment.

## Warranty

New installs have a 10-year parts warranty from the manufacturer when the equipment is registered within 60 days, and a 2-year labor warranty from us. We register the equipment for you.

## Budget

Financing is available for jobs over $2,500, with approved credit.
