title: How much does a leak repair cost?
topic: plumbing
---

Leak repair costs $250 to $900, depending on where the pipe is and how easy it is to reach.

## Active leaks

An active water leak counts as an emergency. Turn off the main water valve while you wait for us. Emergencies get the first open visit today or tomorrow.

## Slow leaks

A slow drip under a sink or a damp spot on a wall is not an emergency, but it gets worse over time. Book a repair visit. A repair visit has an $89 diagnostic fee, and the fee is waived if you approve the repair.

## Warranty

Repairs have a 1-year warranty on parts and labor.
