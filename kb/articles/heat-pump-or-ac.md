title: Should I get a heat pump or an AC with a furnace?
topic: install

Both work well in Austin. The right choice depends on your home.

## Heat pump

A heat pump cools in summer and heats in winter with one outdoor unit. A heat pump system costs $8,000 to $16,000 installed.

## AC with a furnace

A central AC cools, and a furnace heats with gas. A new central AC system with a matching furnace or air handler costs $6,500 to $14,000 installed.

## How to decide

The technician looks at your ductwork, your gas line and the size of your home. Every estimate includes a load calculation. You get a written quote with 3 options, so you can compare.

## Book an estimate

Every install starts with a free in-home estimate. Installs need the property owner to request the estimate.
