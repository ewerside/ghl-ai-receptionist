id: diagnostic-fee
title: How much does a repair visit cost?
topic: pricing
---

A repair visit has an $89 diagnostic fee.

## What the fee covers

The technician finds the problem and gives you a written price before any work starts. You decide whether to go ahead.

## When the fee is waived

If you approve the repair, the $89 fee is waived. You only pay for the repair.

## When there is no fee

- Install estimates are a free in-home estimate, with no diagnostic fee.
- Kestrel Care members pay no diagnostic fee.

## After-hours visits

Visits outside regular hours (evenings, Sundays and holidays) have a $149 after-hours fee on top of the $89 diagnostic fee.
